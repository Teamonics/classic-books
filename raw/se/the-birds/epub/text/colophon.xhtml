Colophon

The Birds
was written in 414 BC by
Aristophanes.
It was translated from Greek in 1912 by
The Athenian Society.

This ebook was produced for
Standard Ebooks
by
Robin Whittleton,
and is based on a transcription produced in 2002 by
Derek Davis, David Widger, and Distributed Proofreaders
for
Project Gutenberg
and on digital scans from
Google Books.

The cover page is adapted from
Concert of Birds,
a painting completed before 1679 by
Paul de Vos.
The cover and title pages feature the
League Spartan and Sorts Mill Goudy
typefaces created in 2014 and 2009 by
The League of Moveable Type.
